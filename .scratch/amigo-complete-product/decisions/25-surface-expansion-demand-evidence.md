# Collect Surface Expansion Demand Evidence

Parent: [Amigo Complete Product](../MAP.md)
Status: open
Label: `wayfinder:task`
Type: HITL / task
Severity: `severity:medium`
Owner: unassigned
Blocked by: [Choose the Channel Expansion Priority](16-channel-expansion-priority.md)

## Question

Collect the observed beta evidence required to determine whether WhatsApp, voice, PWA, or native
mobile has passed its demand gate, without pitching those solutions to participants.

## Completion checklist

- Record otherwise-qualified prospects who refuse or abandon Amigo specifically because Telegram
  is required.
- Record content-free unsupported voice-input attempts and recurring situations where typing blocks
  the Core Loop.
- Record repeated mobile-dashboard use plus installation, push-access, weak-connectivity,
  notification, performance, and accessibility friction.
- Review the evidence after both beta waves against the exact thresholds in
  [Choose the Channel Expansion Priority](16-channel-expansion-priority.md).
- Record which gates passed, which did not, the supporting counts, and the evidence locations.

## Comments

This task is the evidence prerequisite for the WhatsApp, voice, and mobile product decisions. It
does not authorize an expansion; only a passed gate opens the matching decision ticket.
