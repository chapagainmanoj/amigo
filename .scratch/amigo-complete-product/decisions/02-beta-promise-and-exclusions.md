# Lock the Beta Promise and Capability Exclusions

Parent: [Amigo Complete Product](../MAP.md)
Status: closed

Severity: `severity:high`

Blocked by: none

## Question

What single promise should an invited participant hear, which currently shipped behaviors support
it, and which non-clinical, memory, mode, and channel capabilities must beta copy explicitly
exclude?

## Comments

### Resolution — 2026-08-29

#### Canonical beta promise

> **Tell Amigo what you need to do. It turns the conversation into a task and sends you a
> Telegram reminder at the time you choose, with simple Done, Skip, or Later controls.**

This promise describes the demonstrated Core Loop without implying proactive companionship,
persistent ignored-Reminder follow-up, adaptive behavior, durable memory, or multiple channels.

#### Shipped capability contract

Beta copy may present Dashboard Account creation, Telegram Pairing, preferred-name and timezone
setup, conversational Task creation, explicit-date/time Reminder scheduling, Telegram Reminder
delivery, Done/Skip/Later, and dashboard review of Tasks and Reminders. Help, privacy, export, and
deletion support become claimable only after their Gate B paths pass. Recent Sessions are
supporting context, not a marketed feature. Dashboard Task mutation or Snooze is not claimable
until it uses the same verified backend behavior as Telegram.

#### Explicit exclusions

Beta copy must not claim or imply proactive morning/evening or companionship outreach, routine
learning, persistent follow-up after an ignored Reminder, temporal/semantic Memory, Memory
Inspector, adaptive coaching, Recommender/Coach/Reflect, mood journaling, CBT-informed exercises,
clinical functions, WhatsApp, voice, PWA/native mobile, guaranteed delivery or outcomes, human
monitoring, human feelings/consciousness/relationship, or unfunded “coming soon” features.

Diagnosis, treatment, therapy, medical advice, and monitored crisis response remain prohibited by
the map's non-clinical boundary.

#### Category and emotional language

Keep **Amigo** as the brand and use **AI accountability companion** as the beta category. The
interaction may be warm, conversational, and supportive, but copy must not describe Amigo as a
friend who knows or cares about the participant or imply feelings, consciousness, dependence, or
a human relationship. “AI friend” may be reconsidered only with evidence that it improves
understanding without misleading emotional expectations.

#### Dashboard-first explanation

> **Create your Amigo account, connect Telegram, and schedule your first reminder. Telegram is
> where Amigo chats with you and delivers reminders; the dashboard is where you review your
> Tasks, Reminders, settings, and account data.**

The journey explains that the account securely links Telegram and manages stored data; Pairing
links one Dashboard Account to one Telegram Profile; the beta retains data according to its
published policy; Telegram is currently required; and Amigo is experimental, not an emergency or
safety service.

#### “Reaches out first” boundary

Allowed wording: **“Amigo reaches out on Telegram when a Reminder you scheduled becomes due.”**
Copy may explain that participants need not reopen a task app, receive a message at the scheduled
time, and can reply Done, Skip, or Later. It may not claim general check-ins, awareness of need,
routine or mood detection, persistent follow-up until completion, guaranteed availability, or
crisis monitoring until the corresponding behavior has separately passed its release gate.
