# Collect International Crisis-Referral Demand Evidence

Parent: [Amigo Complete Product](../MAP.md)
Status: open
Label: `wayfinder:task`
Type: HITL / task
Severity: `severity:critical`
Owner: unassigned
Blocked by: [Define the Non-Clinical Wellbeing and Crisis-Referral Contract](15-nonclinical-wellbeing-contract.md)

## Question

Collect participant demand and operational prerequisites before deciding whether verified Crisis
Referral coverage should expand beyond the United States and Canada.

## Completion checklist

- Record participant-selected countries and languages that expose a need for localized referral
  coverage, without inferring location.
- Record the number and context of affected participants using content-free evidence.
- For any candidate market, locate official emergency and crisis-referral sources and record their
  verification owner and date.
- Resolve commercial-use terms or permission before treating a third-party directory as an
  eligible source.
- Record the candidate market set, demand evidence, source and permission status, localization
  needs, and evidence locations.

## Comments

This task does not authorize a new support claim. Until the dependent decision closes, only the
United States and Canada have approved localized Crisis Referral coverage.
