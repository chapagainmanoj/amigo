# Collect Recommender Mode Demand Evidence

Parent: [Amigo Complete Product](../MAP.md)
Status: open
Label: `wayfinder:task`
Type: HITL / task
Severity: `severity:medium`
Owner: unassigned
Blocked by: [Choose the Mode and Adaptive Coaching Contract](14-mode-and-adaptation-contract.md)

## Question

Collect evidence of independently demonstrated recommendation problems to determine whether one
narrow domain has passed the Recommender Mode demand gate.

## Completion checklist

- Record recurring participant decision problems without pitching Recommender Mode or candidate
  domains.
- Group only substantively equivalent problems and preserve links to their source evidence.
- Confirm whether at least three independent participants demonstrate the same recurring problem.
- Record the qualifying domain, count, evidence locations, and whether the gate passed.

## Comments

Completing this task opens a domain decision only when the threshold is met. Roadmap interest or a
hypothetical preference does not count as evidence.
