## Question

approved Memory trust contract at beta scale, and what evidence supports that choice over simpler
alternatives?

## Comments

Evaluate technology only after the durable-Memory demand gate passes. The decision must preserve
tenant isolation, provenance, temporal validity, lifecycle history, bounded retrieval, complete
inspection/export/deletion, and prompt-injection resistance.
