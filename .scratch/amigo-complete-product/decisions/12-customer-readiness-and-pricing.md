# Define Customer Readiness and the Sustainable Offer

Parent: [Amigo Complete Product](../MAP.md)
Status: closed
Label: `wayfinder:grilling`

Severity: `severity:high`
Owner: unassigned
Blocked by: [Choose the Invitation Cohort and Recruitment Protocol](11-cohort-and-recruitment.md)

## Question

Which beta outcomes justify Customer Readiness, and which hosted, open-core, managed, free, or
paid offer is sustainable given observed retention, support load, model usage, and channel cost?

## Comments

### Decision 1 — hosted-first commercial model

- The first customer offer is a hosted Amigo subscription operated by the founder. The 30-day
  invitation beta remains free under ticket 03.
- Do not initially offer managed deployments, enterprise installations, supported self-hosting,
  or open-core packaging. Reconsider distribution models only after the hosted Core Loop produces
  retention, customer-demand, cost, and support evidence.
- Remove “completely free and open source” from public copy. The repository has no project license,
  so it is not open source. Source licensing remains a separate human-approved decision; if the
  repository is public before then, describe it accurately as source-visible rather than open
  source.

### Decision 2 — Customer Readiness and commercial validation

- Customer Readiness requires every Gate-C criterion from ticket 01 plus at least five qualifying
  D7 completions, three participants with concrete progress toward their predefined outcome, and
  three participants requesting continued access.
- Offer those participants an unsubsidized paid continuation. At least two must accept before the
  offer is called commercially validated. Fewer acceptances may still support demo readiness, but
  requires iteration rather than a paid-demand claim.
- Average founder product-support burden must be no more than 15 minutes per weekly active
  participant, excluding scheduled research and platform-wide incidents. The Core Loop requires
  no recurring manual founder action.
- Measured variable model, database, and channel cost must be no more than 25% of proposed
  subscription revenue per active subscriber.
- Preserve the existing quality requirements: at least 80% onboarding, 70% Unassisted Activation,
  99% healthy-provider Reminder delivery, zero known duplicate/cross-user Reminders, p95 lateness
  under five minutes, three documented outcomes, self-service data rights, customer-ready copy and
  demo, and the required reviews.

### Decision 3 — provisional Pricing Hypothesis

- Test one hosted plan at US$9 per month, month-to-month. This is a revisable Pricing Hypothesis,
  not a permanent price or customer-facing promise before Customer Readiness.
- Do not introduce a free-forever tier, annual plan, lifetime deal, feature tiers, enterprise
  pricing, or founder/friend discount in the first validation.
- The offer contains only shipped Dashboard Account, Telegram Pairing, conversational Task capture,
  Reminder and Done/Skip/Later behavior, task dashboard, basic settings, data export/deletion, and
  asynchronous product support under documented usage limits.
- Memory, modes, wellbeing, WhatsApp, voice, and mobile Roadmap Capabilities are not included
  promises.
- At this price, variable cost must remain at or below US$2.25 per active subscriber and at least
  two eligible beta graduates must accept the unsubsidized continuation. Recalculate before
  broader selling.

### Decision 4 — payment validation and repricing

- Paid continuation counts only after an eligible participant completes an actual first-month
  payment. A statement of intent is not acceptance.
- Use a reputable hosted checkout/payment link for the first payments; do not build custom billing
  before validation, and do not store card details in Amigo. Provide clear recurring-price
  disclosure, a receipt, self-service cancellation, and account/data choices.
- If fewer than two participants pay US$9, investigate value, positioning, and retention before
  changing price; do not immediately discount and retest the same people.
- Reassess price after 10 paying subscribers or 90 days of paid operation, whichever comes first,
  and sooner if variable cost exceeds 25%, founder support exceeds 15 minutes per weekly active
  subscriber, conversion is weak, or shipped scope materially changes.
- Notify customers of future price changes. Do not imply a lifetime founding price unless one is
  deliberately offered under a later contract.

### Resolution — 2026-08-29

#### Beta-exit decision tree

- When quality, participant value, cost/support sustainability, and paid acceptance all pass,
  declare Customer Readiness and expand the US$9 hosted offer through another measured cohort.
- When quality and value pass but fewer than two participants pay, Amigo may be Demo Ready but is
  not commercially validated. Continue interviews and test positioning/value before changing
  price.
- When quality passes but fewer than three participants demonstrate meaningful outcomes, iterate
  the Core Loop or Target Segment before prioritizing Roadmap Capabilities.
- When demand exists but support or variable cost exceeds its threshold, improve operations,
  limits, and cost before selling; do not hide unsustainable founder labor.
- When reliability, privacy, security, or non-clinical gates fail, Amigo is not Customer Ready and
  selling pauses until the gate passes.
- When retention and outcome evidence remain weak after a second focused cohort, reposition or
  stop the hosted offer rather than adding Memory, modes, or channels to manufacture demand.
- Source licensing remains deferred. Public open-source claims remain prohibited until a separate
  founder-approved license decision is recorded and implemented.
