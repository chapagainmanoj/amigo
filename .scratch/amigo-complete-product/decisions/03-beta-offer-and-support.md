## Question

Will the invitation beta be free or paid, what founder support does it include, what usage limits
apply, and what expectations should participants accept before joining?

## Comments

### Resolution — 2026-08-29

#### Offer

The first 5–10-participant beta is free, invitation-only, and requires no payment card.
Participants exchange structured product feedback and a D7 interview for early access. Pricing
questions and measured operating cost inform a later paid-offer decision; the invitation beta is
not itself a willingness-to-pay test.

#### Duration and usage

- Initial participation lasts 30 days.
- Each participant may use up to 30 conversational Turns, 10 new Tasks, and 10 scheduled Reminders
  per day.
- Messages are limited to 2,000 characters and five Turns per minute.
- Dashboard reads and account/data-management actions do not consume conversational quota.
- Legitimate testing limits may be raised manually when the change is recorded.
- Limit failures are explicit and recoverable; Amigo never silently discards work.

#### Founder support

Participants first attempt onboarding without intervention and may request one optional 20-minute
founder onboarding/research call. Support is asynchronous through `/feedback` and a dedicated
support email, with a one-business-day normal response target. Critical privacy, security, or
duplicate/misdirected Reminder reports are reviewed as soon as reasonably possible. Amigo does not
promise 24/7, emergency, clinical, or crisis support. Affected participants receive direct outage
or enrollment-pause notices. Founder intervention is recorded and assisted Activation does not
count as unassisted success. Support covers product/account/data help, not off-product personal
coaching.

#### Participant expectations

Before joining, a participant acknowledges that they are 18+, use a personal Dashboard Account
and Telegram account, and understand that Amigo is experimental. Reminders may be late, missed,
duplicated, or unavailable. Amigo must not be relied on for medication, emergencies,
safety-critical obligations, therapy, diagnosis, treatment, or crisis response. Data is processed
under the published beta privacy/retention contract. Participants are asked for honest feedback
and baseline/D7 interviews, and must not share accounts, automate high-volume use, probe another
participant's data, or use Amigo for harm or harassment. Participation is voluntary; access or
enrollment may pause for safety, security, reliability, abuse, or cost reasons.

#### Completion, withdrawal, and termination

A participant may withdraw at any time without giving a reason and may request export before
deletion. Withdrawal follows the documented retention/deletion process; it does not silently mean
immediate deletion unless requested. The 30-day period includes a D7 interview and ends with an
explicit choice to extend access, join a waitlist, or close the account. Abuse or a Critical
safety/security incident permits immediate suspension; otherwise founder termination includes
reasonable notice and export/deletion instructions. Beta access does not guarantee permanent free
access, future features, or migration into a paid product. Material changes to limits, data
handling, or the non-clinical boundary require renewed acknowledgment.
