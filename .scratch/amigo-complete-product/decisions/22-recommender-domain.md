## Question

Which independently demonstrated decision problem should Recommender Mode solve first, and which
sources, freshness rules, attribution, preference controls, cost limits, and commercial disclosures
make its recommendations trustworthy and sustainable?

## Comments

Do not select a domain from the roadmap alone. Begin only after at least three independent
participants demonstrate the same recurring recommendation problem.

### 2026-10-03 — Founder hypotheses, not domain approval

The founder proposed contextual everyday choices: clothing, meals, films, leisure, and household
decisions such as laundry using drying equipment and weather. They also raised learning from
behavior or other Modes. This is a broader vision than the approved initial one-domain contract;
no domain, source, behavioral-learning, or cross-Mode inference policy has been approved.

See [Recommender planning inputs](../../mode-expansion-planning/recommender-plan.md) for the
candidate modular architecture and preference/constraint/context distinctions. Independent demand
evidence remains absent; this ticket stays open and its blocker remains unchanged.

### 2026-10-03 — Internal scenario selected

The owner chose weather-aware laundry decisions for the first internal contextual-recommendation
scenario. [Scenario decision and scope](../../mode-expansion-planning/decisions/06-first-recommender-scenario.md).
This does not select a commercial domain or source, prove the three-participant demand gate,
or authorize live Mode registration. This canonical domain/source decision remains open.
