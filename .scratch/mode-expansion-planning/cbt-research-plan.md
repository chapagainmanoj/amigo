## Placement

The commercial candidate portfolio remains Daily, Coach, Reflect, and Recommender. The owner
requested a separate limited CBT research experiment. Working identifier: `cbt_research`, confined
to a dedicated research harness, not the production Mode catalogue or normal `/mode` list.

CBT-informed Thought Check templates are adjacent to Reflect, but they are not a new name for
Reflect and not therapy. Reflect's ordinary debrief/decision/weekly-review job stands on its own.
Any future reuse of a reviewed template needs a separate Reflect release decision and fresh tests;
research results do not automatically validate a commercial capability.

## Binding boundary versus proposed experiment

Binding existing decision: no diagnosis, disorder likelihood, clinical screening/scales, treatment
plans, medical/medication advice, therapist presentation, automated safety plans, dependency claims,
or claims of monitored crisis response. Research-only status does not waive those boundaries.
Actual clinical CBT research is not authorized by this request or the current product destination.

The owner confirmed the focus as **CBT-informed thought examination** and plans to involve a
qualified practitioner; no practitioner or expert-reviewed protocol exists yet.

Revised research question: can bounded AI-adaptive questioning stay within a practitioner-defined
non-clinical thought-examination contract, without inventing facts, pressuring disclosure, imposing
conclusions, or widening Tools? Compare against a fixed sequence on synthetic cases. The fixed
version is a baseline, not an assumed best fit for every scenario. Exact adaptation rules and the
method itself require practitioner review; this is not an approved clinical protocol.

Measure practitioner-rated question appropriateness, adherence to reviewed methods, boundary
violations, stop compliance, privacy, latency, and cost—not symptom improvement, therapeutic
efficacy, or equivalence to a qualified practitioner. [Expert collaboration brief](cbt-expert-brief.md).

The owner selected **founder-only personal experimentation** on 2026-10-03. No beta participants,
customers, friends, or other invitees are included. Synthetic scenarios remain the recommended
first automated tests, not a substitute for this audience decision. This is approval of the planned
audience, not evidence that an implementation is safe or permission to enable an unfinished harness.
Founder-only input can be sensitive and must not bypass the data contract. Any later study with
other people requires a new explicit decision, qualified mental-health review, and a determination
of applicable ethics/institutional/regulatory requirements before recruitment.

## Isolation requirements proposed

- A separate entry point and experiment manifest; no production registry import or customer flag.
- Deterministic default refusal outside the research harness, with tests proving production cannot
  resolve or display `cbt_research`. A prompt saying “research only” is not an access control.
- Synthetic fixtures for automated evaluation; founder-authored personal input only in a separately
  enabled founder-only experiment after its data/safety gates pass. No copied chat logs, participant
  histories, Tasks, Reminders, Coaching Programs, general Memory, mood profiles, or cross-Mode data.
- No persistence or external side-effect Tools, no proactive messages, no purchases or contact,
  and no Daily handoff in the first research harness.
- Do not use the ordinary Turn path if it would persist transcripts. Isolate experiment handling
  and verify model-provider/log/span behavior rather than claiming the ordinary pipeline is private.
- Persist only approved synthetic inputs and content-free run metadata by default. Founder personal
  content is ephemeral in Amigo by default, but that does not imply the model provider retains
  nothing. Explain and approve provider processing/retention before the first personal run. Raw
  founder transcripts are not saved under the selected initial contract; any future retention
  needs a new explicit decision. Do not treat founder ownership as consent to all processing.
- Versioned practitioner-reviewed question patterns and adaptation rules, explicit start/stop,
  bounded Steps and budget, static model-independent refusal/referral fallback, and an immediate
  experiment kill switch. Unrestricted generated exercises or unreviewed clinical methods are out
  of scope; broader adaptation would require a deliberate review of the existing fixed-template
  wellbeing contract rather than a silent relaxation.

The owner selected **no saved personal transcripts; in-memory use only** on 2026-10-03.
The initial personal-use version must not retain founder inputs, generated replies, derived
summaries, or content-bearing logs. Adding saved entries would require a new explicit decision.
This does not establish zero retention by a model provider or authorize personal/model processing;
the exact template, access, provider handling, and safety controls remain open.

## Proposed practitioner / runtime division

The practitioner defines and reviews the method, permitted question patterns, when they apply,
exclusions, and stopping rules. Engineering translates these into enforceable runtime limits and
evaluation cases. AI may select and phrase a permitted follow-up using only current-run input,
without taking authority over consent, boundaries, retention, or completion.

Begin evaluation with synthetic cases and practitioner-reviewed fictional dialogue. Actual founder
transcripts remain unsaved and are not automatically shared for review. Any later supervised
personal-session review needs explicit agreement; data minimization is not waived for evaluation.

## Candidate flow for qualified review, not an approved exercise

The owner selected guided questions rather than predetermined answers for the eventual founder
personal flow. Ask one question at a time; each step is optional and stop is immediate. Do not
correct or grade real personal interpretations using the fictional preview's menu-answer rules.
The outline still needs exact wording and qualified review; personal input is not enabled.

The owner also selected a bounded format: **one everyday situation per run**, ending with an
optional participant-owned takeaway or an earlier stop. A new situation requires a fresh explicit
start, not automatic continuation. This is a format decision, not a numeric model-call budget or
approval of the exact template.

1. Explicitly choose one reviewed Thought Check template; explain experiment limits.
2. State a fictional everyday situation and separate described events from interpretations.
3. Examine the participant-provided thought without inventing motives, denying real harm, or
   treating an alternative interpretation as the correct answer.
4. Offer an optional participant-owned alternative or takeaway; no forced positive reframing.
5. End or stop immediately. No assignment, clinical score, treatment plan, or automatic Task.

Ask one question at a time. Distress, discomfort, safety concerns, or immediate danger interrupt
the exercise; never pressure completion or replace appropriate human help with an exercise.
Exact templates, exclusions, wording, and source/licensing remain to be reviewed.

## Proposed sequence and gates

1. Founder-only audience is selected; approve one non-clinical research question and the exact
   personal-data/provider-processing contract. Keep all other questions out of scope.
2. Obtain review of the exact template/claims and inspect source licensing before reproducing it.
3. Build an isolated synthetic harness and versioned ordinary/adversarial/crisis/privacy fixtures.
4. Prove access isolation, no side effects, stop behavior, template adherence, content-free telemetry,
   truthful uncertainty, and static fallback. Any clinical claim, sensitive leakage, or unauthorized
   side effect fails the run. Do not average a hard violation into a passing score.
5. Run reproducible model comparisons with blinded scoring where feasible; record model/template
   versions and all failures. Small simulated samples cannot establish efficacy or human safety.
6. Permit founder-only personal runs only after the isolation, template, stop, and privacy checks
   pass; identify the founder through trusted harness access, not a model-supplied user identifier.
7. Decide stop, revise, or propose a separately governed study with other people. No automatic
   enrollment or commercial promotion.

## External evidence and limits

WHO's health-AI guidance emphasizes risks, governance, and stakeholder involvement; this supports
qualified review but does not endorse this design. [WHO guidance announcement](https://www.who.int/news/item/18-01-2024-who-releases-ai-ethics-and-governance-guidance-for-large-multi-modal-models).

OHRP explains how research, human-subject involvement, exemptions, funding, and institutional rules
affect review requirements. Calling an activity research does not itself settle those questions;
not every private experiment is automatically subject to the Common Rule. [OHRP training](https://www.hhs.gov/ohrp/education-and-outreach/online-education/human-research-protection-training/lesson-2-what-is-human-subjects-research/index.html).

FDA's January 2026 general-wellness guidance addresses low-risk healthy-lifestyle products; it is
not a blanket exemption or a finding that this CBT experiment qualifies. A qualified assessment
of intended use and claims would be needed for any proposed health/clinical functionality.
[FDA guidance](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/general-wellness-policy-low-risk-devices).
