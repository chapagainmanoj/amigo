# Design question, findings, and approval gaps

Status: throwaway synthetic-only design preview, prepared 2026-10-03; owner verdict pending.

The preview asks whether start/stop/back/exit and an optional takeaway feel clear in a bounded
flow. It does not evaluate therapeutic efficacy, health outcomes, actual personal-input safety,
model adherence, provider retention, or participant acceptability. Scripted menu examples cannot
certify those properties.

## What to observe manually

1. Start, choose either fictional example, choose an unsupported certainty, then the described
   fact. Does the fixed feedback clearly refer only to the fiction?
2. Backtrack from interpretation and choose again. The earlier step must require a fresh answer.
3. Finish with no takeaway. It must be a normal completion, not a failed exercise.
4. Stop from any screen; selected example and answers clear. Restart returns to the notice.
5. Exit from any screen; no Tools, stored record, or notification result appears.

The fixed multiple-choice wording necessarily supplies predetermined options. This deliberately
does **not** establish how a personal flow should handle uncertainty, real harm, alternative
interpretations, or participant-authored wording. Do not adopt those choices as a reviewed template.

## Approvals needed before founder personal-input or live-model work

- [ ] Approve the exact non-clinical research question, exclusions, bounded Steps/budget, and what
  counts as success/failure. No diagnosis, screening, treatment plans, or clinical efficacy claim.
- [ ] Review exact template wording, source/licensing, version, stop/discomfort controls, claims,
  and qualified-review requirement. This original control-flow sketch is not that approval.
- [ ] Approve personal-content handling: ephemeral application state versus any provider retention,
  processors, access, raw-input/log/span/error behavior, consent, deletion/export, and any optional
  retained research record. Founder ownership is not blanket processing permission.
- [ ] Choose trusted founder-only access enforcement and prove production cannot list/resolve or
  run the harness. A menu notice is not an access control.
- [ ] Implement/review model-independent limitation/referral fallback, resource freshness, danger
  overrides, immediate stop, and experiment kill switch. No inferred location or monitoring claim.
- [ ] Build and independently verify synthetic ordinary/adversarial/safety/privacy fixtures and
  hard-failure rules before a founder personal run. The prototype skill intentionally skips tests
  for this throwaway control preview; that does not waive verification for a real harness.
- [ ] Record explicit go/no-go for personal experimentation. No other people, commercial rollout,
  production Mode, or automatic Reflect reuse is included.

## Founder flow direction — 2026-10-03

For the future personal-input version, the founder selected guided questions, one at a time,
with every step optional and immediate stop. The fixed choices here remain a synthetic control
preview only; do not reuse their answer-gating logic to judge or impose real interpretations.
Exact prompts, scope, and provider/safety gates still need resolution before that version is built.

The founder subsequently selected one everyday situation per run, with an optional takeaway and
no indefinite continuation. Completion or early stop ends that run; another situation needs a
fresh explicit start. Numeric Step limits and the real-input/template/provider gates remain open.

The founder clarified the research focus as CBT-informed thought examination with planned
qualified-practitioner involvement. A rigid sequence is not assumed suitable for every scenario;
bounded adaptive questioning is the intended research candidate, with the fixed version as a
comparison baseline. This menu preview cannot establish adherence to expert practice.
