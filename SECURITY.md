# Security Policy

Amigo handles account, conversation, task, and reminder data. Please report suspected
vulnerabilities privately and avoid accessing or retaining data that is not yours.

## Supported Versions

Amigo is in invitation beta and does not yet publish supported release branches.

| Version | Supported |
|---|---|
| Current hosted beta and current default branch | Yes |
| Older commits, deployments, and forks | No |

The open-source license does not make third-party deployments supported Amigo services.

## Report a Vulnerability

Use GitHub's private vulnerability-reporting flow:

<https://github.com/chapagainmanoj/amigo/security/advisories/new>

Do not disclose an unpatched vulnerability in a public issue, discussion, chat, or support
message. If private reporting is temporarily unavailable, do not post exploit details publicly;
retry the private channel after the repository owner restores it.

Include, when available:

- the affected component and revision;
- the observed and expected behavior;
- minimal reproduction steps or a proof of concept;
- the likely impact and prerequisites; and
- any suggested mitigation.

Do not send credentials, conversation content, personal data, or data belonging to another user.
Use synthetic examples and redact logs wherever possible.

## What to Expect

- Target acknowledgement: within three business days.
- Target initial assessment: within seven business days.
- Further updates: when the impact, mitigation, or disclosure plan materially changes.

These are response targets, not guarantees of a fix or a particular resolution date. Please allow
coordinated disclosure until a fix is available or a disclosure date is agreed. Amigo does not
currently operate a bug-bounty program and does not promise payment or broad legal safe harbor.

Reports made in good faith will be evaluated respectfully. Testing must not disrupt service,
degrade availability, use social engineering, or access, modify, retain, or disclose another
person's data.
